Why India works for UAE and Gulf buyers

Short sailing times, a trade agreement already in force and deep capability in hospitality-grade home products make India a natural sourcing base for the Gulf.

Proximity

Days at sea, not weeks

India's west-coast ports are a short sailing from Jebel Ali, which means lower freight cost, smaller safety stock and faster replenishment than sourcing from East Asia.

Trade agreement

Preferential duty under CEPA

The India-UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement removes or reduces UAE customs duty on most Indian goods that meet its rules of origin.

Projects

Hotel, residential and retail supply

Indian factories regularly produce to specification for hospitality and fit-out: bed and bath linen, custom rugs, case goods, lighting and decorative metal.

Duties & trade status

UAE import duty on Indian home goods under CEPA

The agreement has been in force since May 2022. The saving is real, but only if the product qualifies and the paperwork is right.

What CEPA covers

The UAE has committed to duty elimination on more than 97% of its tariff lines, covering about 99% of India's exports by value. Textiles, furniture and handicrafts are among the sectors that benefit.

Rules of origin

Preferential duty applies only to goods that meet the CEPA rules of origin and ship with a valid CEPA certificate of origin. Goods that do not qualify pay the standard UAE duty.

Check your HS code

Coverage is set tariff line by tariff line. We confirm the HS classification and origin position for each item at costing stage so the duty saving is known before you order.

Last reviewed November 2026. Duty rates and trade rules change; confirm the rate for your exact tariff code with your customs broker before costing an order.

UAE requirements, built into the order

We write UAE and GCC requirements into the product brief and packaging specification at the start, so goods are not held at the port for relabelling.

Documentation

CEPA and customs paperwork

The documents your clearing agent will ask for.

  • CEPA certificate of origin from the authorised Indian agency
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matched to HS codes
  • Bill of lading and insurance documents
  • Fumigation or treatment certificates for wood where required
Labelling

Arabic labelling and origin marking

Labelling rules depend on the product category.

  • Arabic or bilingual product labels where regulations require them
  • Country-of-origin marking on product and carton
  • Care and fibre labels for textiles
  • Retailer-specific barcode and carton marks
Conformity

MOIAT and GCC schemes

Regulated products need conformity assessment before sale.

  • Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS) for regulated goods
  • GCC G-Mark for low-voltage electrical items such as lighting
  • Food-contact safety testing for tableware
  • Test reports from accredited labs
Projects

Hospitality and fit-out specifications

Contract supply has its own technical demands.

  • Fire-performance requirements set by the project consultant
  • Custom sizes, colours and logo work approved by sample
  • Phased deliveries aligned to the site programme
  • Pre-shipment inspection reports for the client's records
Where it is made

India's home-product clusters, matched to your range.

Indian home goods are made in specialised regional clusters, and capability varies sharply between them. Choosing the right cluster and the right factory within it decides cost, quality and lead time.

Home textiles & rugs

Panipat for throws, cushions, bath mats and rugs; Karur for kitchen and table linen; Bhadohi and Mirzapur for hand-knotted and hand-tufted carpets; Jaipur for block prints and flatweaves. Read our guides to home textiles and rugs and carpets.

Furniture

Décor, lighting & tabletop

Lead times and shipping from India to the UAE

Short transit is India's biggest advantage for Gulf buyers. These are indicative ranges; we confirm them factory by factory.

Development

Sampling: 3 to 6 weeks

Depends on category and how much is custom. Project-specific rugs and furniture take longest; repeat textile constructions are fastest.

Production

Bulk: 8 to 14 weeks

From approved sample and confirmed order. Handmade categories and the weeks before Diwali need extra margin.

Transit

Sea freight: about a week

Sailing from Mundra or Nhava Sheva to Jebel Ali is typically under a week, plus port handling at both ends. Mixed loads from several factories can be consolidated into one container.

FAQ

Sourcing from India for the UAE: common questions

Short answers to the questions UAE buyers ask us most often.

Is there customs duty on home goods imported from India to the UAE?

Under the India-UAE CEPA, in force since May 2022, the UAE has committed to removing duty on more than 97% of its tariff lines. Goods must meet the rules of origin and ship with a CEPA certificate of origin to qualify, and coverage depends on the exact HS code.

How long does shipping from India to Dubai take?

Sea transit from India's west-coast ports to Jebel Ali is typically under a week, plus handling time at both ports. Production lead time is usually the longer part of the calendar.

Can you supply hotel and fit-out projects?

Yes. We source to specification for hospitality and residential projects, including bed and bath linen, custom rugs, furniture, lighting and decorative items, with samples approved before bulk production.

Can goods be re-exported from Dubai to other markets?

Many UAE importers use Dubai as a distribution hub for the Gulf and Africa. Duty treatment for re-exports depends on how and where the goods are cleared, so plan this with your clearing agent before shipment.

Can you consolidate products from several factories?

Yes. We plan consolidation so items from different clusters ship together, which suits mixed home ranges and smaller quantities per product.

Tell us what you want to source.

Share your category, target price, volumes and destination. We will come back with a view on the right clusters, realistic lead times and how we would run the programme.
