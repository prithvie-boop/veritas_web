Sourcing glassware from India

From decorative vessels to drinkware: manufacturing methods, breakage control, food contact and Firozabad’s glass cluster.

Updated 4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By Veritas Consulting

What Firozabad is known for

Firozabad, Uttar Pradesh is a recognized glassware cluster, identified by the state's ODOP program and EPCH. It is an efficient place to look for decorative glass, lighting components and glassware development. However, do not assume that a factory making decorative vases is approved for food-contact drinkware. Request evidence for the exact furnace, forming, finishing and decoration route.

Compare mouth-blown, molded and machine-assisted products for shape consistency, tooling cost, surface character and capacity. Clarify whether the item is soda-lime or another glass formulation; claims such as borosilicate, oven-safe or dishwasher-safe need separate substantiation.

A usable glass specification

Set drawings for volume, height, rim and base dimensions, wall-thickness range, weight, color, transparency, bubble/seed allowance and decoration. Approve a physical master sample and a defect board for acceptable handmade variation. Ask for annealing controls, stress inspection, rim finish, thermal-shock tests where relevant and stability checks for vessels or lamps. Specify packaging by individual cell, inner carton, master carton and pallet; run representative transit/drop tests before quoting a low breakage rate.

Food contact is a separate decision

For EU food-contact articles, the framework Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and good manufacturing practice apply, alongside relevant product and national rules. Decorative-only glass should be sold and labeled as decorative only if it has not been validated for food contact. In the US, FDA scrutiny of lead and cadmium in foodwares makes composition and decoration especially important. Ask an accredited laboratory for tests matched to the finished surface and destination market; a generic “food-safe” statement is not sufficient.

Costs, suppliers and 2026–27 outlook

Calculate landed cost including tool/mold charges, minimum color run, rejection allowance, carton density and replacement policy. EPCH reported 2025–26 exports of its glasswares handicraft category below the prior year. That category does not cover every glass product. Our reading: buyers will put more weight on repeatable finishing and packaging evidence than on a low sample price.
