A Closer Look

Frequently Asked Questions

How Veritas brings clarity, control, and accountability to sourcing from India.

Why choose India for home sourcing?

India offers deep craftsmanship & material expertise, competitive manufacturing costs, growing compliance maturity, and scalable production capabilities. But it requires local expertise, systems, and control—that's where we come in.

How does Veritas ensure quality control?

Quality is embedded from pre-production to final shipment. We conduct pre-production quality planning, in-line & mid-line inspections, final inspections & shipment approvals, plus root-cause analysis with corrective action plans.

What product categories do you specialize in?

We cover the entire Home spectrum: Hard Goods (furniture, lighting, décor, glass & ceramics) and Soft Goods (bed & bath textiles, floor coverings, window treatments, kitchen linen).

What makes Veritas different from other sourcing agents?

We don't believe in short-term wins. We build long-term sourcing partnerships. Our philosophy: Strong sourcing is built on process, not people-dependence. Quality must be engineered, not inspected at the end.
