How Veritas works with you

The right supplier for the right product.

01 / Match

We find the right fit

We begin with your product, construction, price point, volume and destination market. Then we identify suitable manufacturing capabilities in India and qualify suppliers for the actual brief, rather than sending a generic catalog.

02 / Develop

We turn the brief into a production plan

We coordinate comparable quotations, product development and samples, then align the approved reference, material specification, quality criteria, packaging and delivery milestones with the supplier.

03 / Execute

We stay involved through shipment

Our India-based team follows production progress, in-line and final quality checks, corrective actions and shipment coordination. You have a clear point of contact when decisions need to be made.

Tell us what you want to source.

Share your product images or drawings, materials, target market, quantities, price range and launch date. We’ll discuss a practical supplier and quality-control route with you.

Why buyers work with Veritas

The sample, price and delivery need to hold up in production.

Finding a factory is only the beginning. The approved sample must be translated into repeatable bulk quality; quoted costs and delivery milestones need clear ownership. Veritas matches the right supplier to the right product, coordinates merchandising and specifications, and stays close to production so issues can be raised and resolved early.

For international buyers in 2026, material traceability and destination-market testing add more decisions to every order. We bring supplier selection, quality assurance and shipment coordination into one working process with an India-based team accountable to you.
